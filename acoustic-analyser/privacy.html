Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the "Acoustic Analyser" mobile application (the "App") created by Bas Spijkerman. This page is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our App.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Acoustic Analyser does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information.

The App requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Microphone Access: The App requires access to your device's microphone to perform acoustic analysis in real-time. The audio data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers, recorded, or shared with third parties.

3. Local Storage

The App may store your settings, preferences, and local analysis data directly on your device using local storage mechanisms (such as SharedPreferences). This data remains on your device and is not accessible by us.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the email provided on the App Store page or through our GitHub repository.
